## Multiple Perspectives
**China's view**: Taiwan is part of Chinese territory, and unification is inevitable. Military exercises are justified warnings against foreign interference.

**Taiwan's view**: It is an independent country with its own government, military, and democracy. It has the right to choose its own future and maintain international relationships.

**U.S. perspective**: Taiwan is a strategic ally and democracy that must be defended, but direct conflict with China is unacceptable. The U.S. maintains ambiguity to deter both Chinese aggression and Taiwanese independence moves.

**Australia's dilemma**: We depend on China economically but are allied with the U.S. militarily. A China-Taiwan conflict would force an impossible choice.

## Future Implications

Even without invasion, ongoing military exercises and economic coercion could gradually force Taiwan's capitulation. Alternatively, Taiwan could strengthen its defences and international support, making invasion too costly for China.
