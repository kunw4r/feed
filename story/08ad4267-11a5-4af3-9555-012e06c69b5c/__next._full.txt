Saudi Arabia built its entire economy on oil since the 1930s. When oil prices collapsed in 2014-2016, the government realised it needed to change. Vision 2030 was created to prepare for a future where oil is less important. The plan includes massive projects (NEOM, Saudi Aramco IPO, entertainment venues) and aims to attract foreign investment and tourism.

## Multiple Perspectives
**Saudi government**: Vision 2030 is progressing; major projects are underway. Diversification takes time. The economy is gradually shifting.
**International investors**: Concerned about slow progress, cost overruns, and whether Saudi Arabia can truly reduce oil dependency. Question project viability.
**Saudi citizens**: Frustrated by rising costs of living, taxes, and lack of private sector jobs despite government spending. Feel benefits aren't reaching them.
**Global energy analysts**: Believe Saudi Arabia is too dependent on oil and won't successfully diversify before global oil demand peaks (possibly 2030s).

- If diversification fails, Saudi Arabia faces economic crisis as oil demand declines

- NEOM and other megaprojects could become white elephants (expensive, unused projects)
