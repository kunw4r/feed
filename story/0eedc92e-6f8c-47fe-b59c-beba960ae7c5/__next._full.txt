In February 2026, opposition parties are fractured and weakened after years of failed attempts to remove Maduro. The international community—including the US, EU, and several Latin American countries—refuses to recognise Maduro as legitimate, but he maintains control through military loyalty and state security forces.

Why this matters: Venezuela sits on the world's largest proven oil reserves, but its oil industry has collapsed due to mismanagement and lack of investment. This affects global energy markets. Additionally, Venezuelan refugees are spreading throughout the Americas, creating migration crises in neighbouring countries and straining international relations.

The root cause: Venezuela's economy was built entirely on oil revenue. When oil prices collapsed in 2014-2016, the government couldn't pay for imports or services. Rather than diversify the economy, Maduro's government printed money (causing hyperinflation), seized private businesses, and blamed external enemies. This destroyed the economy and created mass suffering.

**Neighbouring Countries**: Overwhelmed by Venezuelan refugees; want stability but can't afford to host millions of migrants.

Oil production may never recover without massive foreign investment (which won't happen under Maduro). The refugee crisis will continue destabilising the region.
