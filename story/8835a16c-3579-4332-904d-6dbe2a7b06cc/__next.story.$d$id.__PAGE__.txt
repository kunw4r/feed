The Russia-Ukraine conflict began in 2014 when Russia annexed Crimea. The 2022 full-scale invasion killed over 500,000 people and displaced millions. Previous peace attempts (Minsk agreements 2014-2015) failed because neither side trusted the other to honour agreements. This is the first serious negotiation attempt since the invasion began.

## Multiple Perspectives
**Ukraine's position**: Territorial integrity is non-negotiable. Accepting Russian control of eastern regions sets a precedent that invasion works—other authoritarian states might follow. Ukraine also demands reparations (estimated at $486 billion) to rebuild.

**Global South perspective**: Many nations (India, Brazil, Indonesia) want peace regardless of territorial outcomes, as the war disrupts food and energy supplies worldwide.

## Future Implications
If negotiations succeed: Europe stabilises, sanctions might ease, reconstruction begins. If they fail: NATO may increase military support to Ukraine, risking direct confrontation with Russia. A frozen conflict (like Korea) is also possible—ceasefire without peace treaty.
