## Multiple Perspectives

**Citizens**: Some support privacy protections but worry about government surveillance replacing corporate surveillance.

**Western governments**: View regulations as protectionist and designed to give Chinese companies advantages in domestic markets.

## Future Implications
China's tech sector may become less innovative and globally competitive. However, the government may develop state-controlled AI systems that serve surveillance and control purposes. This could accelerate a "tech decoupling" between China and the West, with separate ecosystems and standards.
