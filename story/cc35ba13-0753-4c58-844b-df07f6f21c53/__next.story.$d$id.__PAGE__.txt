3:T623,## Historical Context
Government spending cuts are politically contentious. The 2013 US government shutdown and sequestration (automatic spending cuts) caused economic disruption. Historically, cutting spending during economic uncertainty can trigger recession because government spending supports jobs and demand.

## Multiple Perspectives
**Trump Administration**: Government is bloated and inefficient. Cuts will reduce debt, eliminate waste, and free up resources for tax cuts that stimulate the private economy.

**Democrats/Progressive Republicans**: Cuts will harm education, healthcare, infrastructure, and vulnerable populations. Government spending supports millions of jobs and is essential during economic uncertainty.

**Fiscal Hawks**: The US national debt ($34+ trillion) is unsustainable. Spending cuts are necessary, though they should be targeted and gradual.

**State/Local Governments**: Federal funding supports schools, infrastructure, and services. Cuts will force states to raise taxes or cut services.

## Future Implications
If cuts proceed: potential job losses in government and contractor sectors, reduced services, possible recession, increased state/local budget crises, and political backlash. Negotiations will likely result in partial cuts targeting specific areas.

- Congressional response and which departments face cuts
- Impact on government contractor companies (many Australian firms work with US government)
